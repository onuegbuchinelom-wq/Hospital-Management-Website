Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment page to choose your doctor, preferred date, and time slot. You'll receive a confirmation email and SMS reminder.

How can I reschedule my appointment?

Open your patient dashboard, go to "My Appointments," and select the appointment you want to change. Choose a new date and time.

Can I cancel without a fee?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the appointment are free. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.

Need immediate help?

Doctors

Where can I see doctor specialties?

Visit the Doctors page to browse specialists by department and expertise. Each profile includes qualifications, experience, and patient reviews.

How do I choose the best doctor?

Compare experience, specialty, patient reviews, and availability. You can filter by department and view ratings from other patients.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, select the "Telehealth" or "Online Consultation" option when booking your appointment for a remote video visit.

Patient Login

How do I log in to my account?

Enter your email and password on the Patient Login page. If you're a new patient, click "Sign Up" to create an account.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email to receive a reset link. Contact support if you need additional help.

Is my personal data secure?

Yes, patient information remains private and protected in the portal. We use industry-standard encryption to safeguard your data.
