PATIENT AND CARE SERVICES

Comprehensive Medical Services

At NeloCare, we provide comprehensive medical services to address a wide range of health conditions. Our team of experienced healthcare professionals is dedicated to delivering personalized care and effective treatment plans for our patients.

Cardiology

Cardiovascular Conditions

We offer specialized care for cardiovascular conditions such as hypertension, coronary artery disease, heart failure, and arrhythmias. Our cardiology team provides comprehensive evaluations, diagnostic testing, and personalized treatment plans to help patients manage their heart health effectively.

Hypertension Heart Disease Arrhythmias
Endocrinology

Diabetes Management

Our diabetes management program focuses on helping patients control their blood sugar levels through lifestyle modifications, medication management, and regular monitoring. We provide education and support to empower patients in managing their diabetes effectively.

Type 1 & 2 Diabetes Gestational Diabetes
Pulmonology

Respiratory Conditions

We treat a variety of respiratory conditions including asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and pneumonia. Our pulmonology team offers comprehensive care to help patients breathe easier and improve their respiratory health.

Oncology

Cancer Care

Our oncology department provides comprehensive cancer care including diagnosis, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and surgical interventions. We offer supportive care to help patients throughout their treatment journey.

Orthopedics

Orthopedics

Our orthopedic specialists treat bone, joint, and muscle conditions including fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, and joint replacements. We offer both surgical and non-surgical treatment options.

SPECIALIZED SERVICES

Advanced Specialized Care

In addition to our core medical services, we also offer specialized care in areas such as oncology, orthopedics, neurology, and pediatrics. Our multidisciplinary teams work together to provide comprehensive care for patients with complex medical conditions.

Oncology

Our oncology department provides comprehensive care for patients with cancer, including diagnosis, treatment, and supportive care. We offer a range of services including chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and surgical interventions to help patients manage their condition effectively.

Chemotherapy Radiation Therapy Surgical Oncology

Neurology

Our neurology department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of neurological disorders such as stroke, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, and Parkinson's disease. We provide comprehensive care to help patients manage their neurological conditions effectively.

Stroke Care Epilepsy Movement Disorders
